## A1.1.1 CPU/Central Processing Unit

Computers are programmable and can be set to perform different tasks without changing any hardware.

Modern computers are **Von Neumann** Machines, they contain:

- **Memory** - store data & instruction
- **CPU** - read data & execute instruction

**Von Neumann** Machines follow **Input-Processing-Output/IPO** model
